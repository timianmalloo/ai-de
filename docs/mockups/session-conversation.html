    “Chat-like”, measured on this page
    
  
    The operator’s word is a feeling; the contract is a set of numbers read from the rendered layout on every change, at the shell’s startup size (1440 × 900) with the session maximized. Rhythm: the editor’s top edge is read at 1, 5 and 40 turns in one pass and must not move. Alignment: every turn’s text, reply and outcome share one left edge. Density: how many turns the tree shows, against a threshold the row prints. The absence of form chrome: no bordered field other than the editor, no box around a turn, no per-prompt settings field.
