Revised for what's actually in ai-de and ai-forward: the run opens with a CT19 goal block, every governed lane lives in its own worktree, budgets are quota vectors rather than dollars, the coordination board shows artifact classes and leases, and the new Agent Profiler renders capacity, consumption, discipline, and live Weave posture per lane.

1 — Starting a run: the goal block is the front door

The composer is a goal-block editor. The conductor drafts it; you edit; a governed sub-agent cannot exist without one. Tier gates ceremony (a T0 run skips council and plan). Worktree chips show where each lane physically lives; the spend meter is a quota vector across your accounts, not a dollar figure.

2 — The coordination board

Contention is a property of the artifact: the class registry (seeded by the extractors) marks generated surfaces "regenerate, don't merge", registers get single-writer leases, and only authored files need track-exclusive ownership. Decisions live on the message board with the conductor as arbiter; the serial spine is named and watched.
