Durable grain and history

One fact is one extractor's normalized subject/predicate/object assertion at one artifact revision. WorkspaceSchema.cs contains dimensions (workspace_dim, node_dim, session_dim), append-only scope/receipt/dispatch/prompt facts and an explicitly rebuildable claim_current_cache. Update/delete triggers protect immutable tables. Scope generation and core epoch gate commits. Separate watcher observations and session/envelope journals are not merged into the code-fact schema by assumption.

The proposed factory WorkItem/Criterion/Assessment/Decision model is Inferred future design in the proposal, not a recovered class or a new implemented table. Source/test facts do not establish semantic acceptance solely because they can be linked.
