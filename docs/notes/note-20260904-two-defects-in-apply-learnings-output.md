# Two defects in `/apply-learnings` output, and one measurement that needed no action

Found reviewing the fleet results applied on 2026-09-04 (`71747f4`). **Neither defect is in this
repository**, and neither is patched here: `docs/ai-forward-pack/` was rewritten wholesale by the
r45 → r59 update, so a fix in the vendored copy is discarded by the next one.

## Defect 1 — a TODO wearing a control's badge

Eight of the eleven inherited classes carry this as their control:

> **Control:** Derive a falsifiable control for this class and observe it failing on the un-fixed
> shape (CI6); move status -> controlled. **(automated control)**

That is an instruction to write a control, labelled as one. It is the exact shape the register exists
to catch — a claim of verification where none exists (DC-016) — generated by the tool that
distributes the register's own rules, and it will propagate to every repository `/apply-learnings`
touches.

The text is not wrong as *guidance*; the parenthetical is the defect. **Suggested fix:** emit
`(no control yet — derive one)` for a class whose control is the placeholder, and reserve
`(automated control)` for a named, runnable check.

## Defect 2 — ids truncated mid-word at 20 characters

`PACK-E-AN-AMBIGUOUS-`, `PACK-D-AN-ARRAY-PARA`, `SHELL-A-CONTENT-ROUT`, `TWO-OR-MORE-AGENT-SE`.
Trailing hyphens and severed words read as corruption rather than abbreviation, and two of them are
no longer greppable back to their source class.

Related: `TWO-OR-MORE-AGENT-SE`'s title is a full paragraph, and `Confidence:` renders as a single
letter (`v`, `i`) — presumably `verified` / `inferred` truncated to one character.

**Suggested fix:** truncate on a word boundary and keep the class prefix intact, or carry the source
id verbatim and put the sentence in the title where it belongs.

## The measurement that needed no action

DC-088's new instance reads: *3 `COORD-NOT-CHECKED-IDENTITY` events under agent `anon`, against 50
allowed and 2 refused — the not-checked path was more common than the refusal it replaces.*

That looked like an argument for making the identity check enforcing. Checked before acting, and it
is not, for two reasons.

**The claim path already refuses.** `coord-core.py` returns exit 4 with
`COORD-NOT-CHECKED-IDENTITY` when `AGENT_SESSION` is unset on `claim` and `release`. Only the
`precommit` hook degrades to advisory, and the pack states why in the code: *"a floor that refuses
all of them is a floor that gets deleted rather than adopted."* That is a considered trade, not an
oversight.

**And this repository's half is already done.** `WorkbenchShell` sets `AGENT_SESSION` unconditionally
for every terminal it launches, with the same trade recorded at the assignment and an observed
incident behind it — a squash merge that swept another session's entire pack refresh into a commit
describing only the merger's work. The remaining `anon` events come from sessions launched *outside*
AI-DE, which the product cannot reach.

So the measurement **confirms the existing fix matters** rather than indicating new work. Worth
saying plainly, because "the fleet measured a gap" reads as a call to action and acting on this one
would have meant reversing a documented trade on a sample of three.

## Residual

The two generator defects are unfixed until someone changes `ai-forward`. Until then, the inherited
section of this repo's register contains eight controls that do not exist and are labelled as though
they do. The section's own header says its classes are "a shape to watch for, not something already
observed here", which limits the damage but does not remove it.
